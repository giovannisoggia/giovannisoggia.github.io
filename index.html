About

I am an economist at the Bank of Italy, where I currently serve as Substitute Head of the Regional Economic Analysis and Research Division at the Cagliari Branch. I joined the Bank of Italy in 2012 and have served as Substitute Head of Division since 2022.

My research interests include banking, corporate finance, entrepreneurship and regional economics. My work combines academic research and policy analysis, with applications to bank supervision, credit markets, innovation and regional development.

Since 2024, I have also been pursuing a part-time PhD in Economics at the Université de Namur.
